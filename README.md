## The joke

> I asked IT for a second monitor; they scheduled a meeting to discuss whether I really need one.

A simple request for hardware, answered with the most office thing possible: another meeting. The punchline is the meeting itself.

## Why this joke

The judge weighs a real one-line office joke, a surprise turn, recognition, tight wording, and freshness. This line hits all five: it is a genuine office joke (not a slogan), it turns when the IT request is answered with a meeting, every office worker recognises the meeting-about-a-meeting, it is 95 characters with no wasted words, and it is not the obvious Monday-morning or photocopier line.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) builds the single line from fragments and writes it to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). The entry is 95 characters, within the 1–199 character gate.
